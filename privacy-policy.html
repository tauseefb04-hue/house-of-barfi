Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

House of Barfi ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit houseofbarfi.com or place an order, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Order information: name, shipping address, email address, and phone number when you place an order.
  • Payment information: we do not collect or store your card details ourselves. Payments are processed securely by Stripe, and are subject to Stripe's own privacy policy.
  • Contact information: if you email or call us, we keep a record of that correspondence to help with your order.

How we use your information

  • To prepare, package, and ship your order
  • To contact you about your order (confirmations, shipping updates, or questions)
  • To improve our products and website

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Who we share information with

We share order details only with the services needed to fulfill your order: Stripe (payment processing) and our shipping carrier (to deliver your package). Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing how they handle your data.

Data retention

We keep order records for as long as reasonably necessary for accounting, tax, and customer service purposes.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we have about you, or to request that we delete it, subject to any recordkeeping we're legally required to keep.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
