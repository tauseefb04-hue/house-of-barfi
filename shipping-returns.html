Shipping & Returns

Last updated: September 2026

Shipping

We ship nationwide across the United States. Every order is made fresh and packed with care to travel well.

  • Processing time: orders are freshly prepared and typically ship within 2 business days of being placed.
  • Carrier: orders are shipped via USPS Priority Mail, which typically takes 2-3 business days in transit after shipping.
  • Shipping cost: a flat $19.99 per order, shown at checkout.

Because our sweets are fresh and perishable, we recommend refrigerating certain items upon arrival and enjoying them within the timeframe noted on your packing slip.

Returns & refunds

Due to the perishable, made-to-order nature of our products, all sales are final. We're unable to accept returns or offer refunds once an order has been placed.

Please review your order carefully before checking out. If you have questions about an item before ordering, reach out and we're happy to help.

Order issues

If your order arrives damaged in transit or something seems wrong, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery at hello@houseofbarfi.com or (929) 461-1044 with a photo of what you received — we want to make it right where we can.
